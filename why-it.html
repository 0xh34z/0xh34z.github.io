Why IT and Why HZ?

I started out hosting Minecraft servers when I was thirteen. That quickly turned into self hosting on homelabs, daily driving Linux, and doing Hack The Box challenges. Given that background, choosing IT was obvious.

The Golden Circle

Simon Sinek's Golden Circle, filled in for myself:

Circle My answer
Why Security is a fundamental part of IT. It's the only reason we can use the internet we all know and love to this day.
How Break it first, then understand it under the hood. Minecraft servers, Linux daily, a Proxmox homelab, and Hack The Box.
What The forensic search tool, this website, my Hack The Box write-ups, other websites.

What I Know of the Field

My first real look at professional IT work was my internship at Politie Zeeland, Digitaal Opsporen, from August 2025 to January 2026. I analyzed seized phones so I got to see how that work actually runs rather than reading about it.

For the internship I also built a proof of concept tool that lets investigators search seized phone databases in plain language, instead of digging through raw database dumps by hand. I wrote about how it works on my own site: AI database investigator.

What I Expect the Work to Look Like

Before my internship I expected the job to be pretty boring. The only real thing I had going for me was a year of MBO level 4. Interning at the national police with only an MBO level 4 felt really unlikely in my head. After I landed the internship (which I was surprised about) I just didn't expect to be allowed to do a lot. I simply didn't have the proper education to work in such a serious DIFR environment. I was really lucky that I just knew a bit of cybersecurity and its DIFR branch.

Why HBO ICT at HZ?

I came to this with an MBO 4 degree in Expert IT systems and devices, plus the internship above. Before HZ I started Applied Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at BUas in Breda. I left during the first weeks, and it was not a disagreement with the subject. It was the three hours of travel, twice a week, and a study style built around reading very large amounts of text. I will read theory for as long as it is genuinely interesting, but not because it was assigned.

HZ in Middelburg fits what I need:

  • It's in Zeeland, which is the difference between three hours of travel a week and a fifteen-minute bicycle ride.
  • Tuition is €2700 a year against the €3650 I was paying at BUas, which over four years is a difference worth having.
  • It gives me the opportunity to earn an HBO diploma while still leaving room for my own projects.

What I Want to Learn and Achieve

Most of what I know was self-taught, which makes it practical but uneven. The programme should give the missing structure. Concretely, I want object oriented design, writing tests, and working with Git in a team, plus the skill of explaining a technical result to someone who does not code, because at the police the tool was worth nothing if I could not explain it to an investigator. In my free time I am responsible for specialization. I know I'm capable of learning whatever I'm interested in, so specialization will not be a problem.

Long Term Plan

Five years from now I want both the HBO ICT diploma and the certifications that get me where I want to be. Which certification depends on where that minor leads: OSAI if I go into offensive AI security, or a pentesting certification such as OSCP or HTB CPTS if I stay in traditional security work.
