Study Direction: Software Engineering vs Data Science

15-10-2026 by Kasper van de Kimmenade

At HZ you must choose between different directions in the IT programme. The two ones you can choose from are Software Engineering and Data Science.

Looking at Both Options

Data Science is heavily focused on numbers, data analysis, and machine learning models. I already tested this area by following the Applied Data Science & Artificial Intelligence course at Breda University of Applied Sciences. But I've also done countless projects with AI myself: fine-tuning a YOLO model to recognize chess pieces and building RAG pipelines. It is cool tech, but pure data science involves a lot of data cleaning, statistical proofs, and analysis that I find less engaging than building software. Also I just think the Data Engineer side is more appealing, Data Scientist is too theoretical for me I think, and Analytics Translator is too corporate, too much Power BI, too many graphs etc for me to handle.

Software Engineering is about writing applications, organizing databases, creating APIs, and deploying services. It deals with real world architecture, security, and making sure code actually runs reliably in production.

Why I Chose Software Engineering

I prefer building tools that people can use directly. During my police internship, the useful part was not just having a model, but writing the FastAPI backend, making SQLite queries, and hooking it up to a clean chat interface. Software Engineering gives me the foundation to build complete systems and secure them properly.
