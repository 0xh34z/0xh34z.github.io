Goals and SWOT Reflection

15-10-2026 by Kasper van de Kimmenade | Goals

This post covers my SWOT analysis, my mission statement, and my two SMART goals.

Mission Statement

My mission is to complete the HBO ICT programme and turn my background into a bachelor degree. I want to build software systems that stay secure, or be the one to break the systems.

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Skills in Linux, Python (depending on what library), networking, and server administration.
  • Troubleshooting bugs or solving HTB labs.
  • Experience working on a practical software project at Politie Zeeland.

Weaknesses

  • Sort of bored by repetitive documentation or purely theoretical coursework.
  • I'm likely to get caught in technical side rabbit holes rather than school deliverables.
  • Asking for help early when stuck.

Opportunities

  • HZ group projects with hopefully: collaboration and good feedback.
  • High demand worldwide in cybersecurity.
  • Third year internship and minor to specialize in offensive security.

Threats

  • Spending too much time on personal side projects at the expense of study deadlines.
  • Poor time management during crowded exam weeks leading to unnecessary resits.
  • Fewer local ICT meetups and companies in Zeeland, so I need to travel or work online for events and internships.

Two SMART Goals and SWOT Links

Goal 1 (from a Weakness): Pass all 60 EC of year one with zero resits

  • Specific: hand in every course deliverable.
  • Measurable: 60 of 60 EC completed with no resits by the end of block 4, visible on the dashboard.
  • Achievable: I already have the technical base from MBO, so my real risk is output.
  • Relevant: No credits means no minor, no internship, no degree.
  • Time-bound: Each deliverable submitted before the deadline.

SWOT links: it directly attacks procrastination on documentation, side rabbit holes instead of deliverables, side projects and crowded exam weeks causing resits.

Goal 2 (from a Strength): Prove the offensive security skill.

  • Specific: Root Hack The Box machines and publish a short write-up of fun ones on kaspervandekimmenade.nl.
  • Measurable: 20 machines owned, of which at least 8 are medium or harder and rooted without walkthroughs, by the end of August 2027.
  • Achievable: I already spend hours on labs and stuck machines, so this formalizes existing time.
  • Relevant: the goal feeds straight into the certification track and the year three internship.
  • Time-bound: at least 2 owned machines per month, checked monthly.

SWOT links: it builds on my hands-on skills and persistence. It replaces claims with a public proof-of-work portfolio for the internship.
