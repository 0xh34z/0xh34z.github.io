PCO Course Site

Hi, I am Kasper, eighteen years old from Zeeland, and this is my PCO site for HBO ICT at HZ. I already maintain a full portfolio and blog at kaspervandekimmenade.nl, so this site exists specifically to document my study requirements and course deliverables.

ICT suits me because I learn by building and breaking things: running Linux daily, self-hosting on Proxmox, and digging into Hack The Box labs until I understand how a system really works. That hands-on troubleshooting, from my Minecraft servers at thirteen to my Politie Zeeland project, is exactly how I want to study.
